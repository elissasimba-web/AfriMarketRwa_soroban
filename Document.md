AfriMarketRwa Project Document
1. Project Title
AfriMarketRwa – Decentralized Marketplace Infrastructure for African Services
2. Introduction
Digital services across Africa are growing rapidly, but many industries still face problems related to trust, transparency, and fragmented platforms.
Users often rely on multiple disconnected systems for renting property, booking transport, accessing agricultural markets, or scheduling healthcare services.
AfriMarketRwa introduces a decentralized marketplace infrastructure that connects multiple service sectors into a single ecosystem powered by Stellar blockchain and Soroban smart contracts.
The platform uses escrow-based payments to ensure secure and transparent transactions between users.
3. Problem Statement
Many digital service platforms in Africa face several key challenges:
Lack of Trust
Users often pay for services before they are delivered, which exposes them to scams or fraud.
Fragmented Platforms
Different services such as travel booking, rental platforms, agriculture markets, and healthcare operate independently without integration.
Payment Risks
Without escrow systems, both buyers and sellers risk financial loss during transactions.
Limited Market Access
Farmers and small businesses struggle to connect with buyers or service providers efficiently.
These challenges limit the growth of digital commerce and cross-sector collaboration.
4. Proposed Solution
AfriMarketRwa addresses these issues by introducing a decentralized marketplace infrastructure that connects multiple industries through smart contracts.
The platform integrates:
Rental services
Tourism booking
Transport services
Healthcare services
Agricultural marketplaces
All transactions are secured using escrow smart contracts on the Stellar blockchain.
Funds are locked during transactions and released only when service conditions are fulfilled.
This creates a trustless transaction environment that protects both buyers and service providers.
5. Platform Architecture
AfriMarketRwa uses a modular architecture where multiple service modules interact with a shared escrow infrastructure.
Core components include:
Escrow Smart Contract
Handles secure payments and transaction settlement.
Rental Module
Allows property owners to rent or sell properties securely.
Tourism Module
Enables booking of tours and travel experiences.
Transport Module
Supports logistics, travel tickets, and delivery services.
Health Module
Facilitates healthcare payments and appointment scheduling.
Agriculture Module
Connects farmers with buyers and agricultural supply markets.
All modules rely on the escrow contract to manage payments securely.
6. Example Service Interaction
The platform allows services to interact with each other.
Example scenario:
A farmer lists crops on the Agriculture marketplace.
A buyer purchases the crops and pays through escrow.
Transport services are used to deliver the crops.
Once delivery is confirmed, the escrow contract releases the payment to the farmer.
This interconnected ecosystem allows services to collaborate seamlessly.
7. DAO Governance
AfriMarketRwa will introduce DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization) governance to ensure fair and decentralized decision-making.
The DAO allows community members, service providers, and stakeholders to participate in governance decisions.
Examples of DAO decisions include:
Marketplace rules
Escrow dispute resolution
Platform fees
Penalty rules in rental agreements
Service provider verification standards
DAO governance ensures that the platform evolves through community participation rather than centralized control.
8. Technology Stack
The platform is built using modern Web3 technologies:
Blockchain: Stellar
Smart Contracts: Soroban
Programming Language: Rust
Payment Asset: USDC on Stellar
These technologies provide:
Fast transactions
Low fees
High security
Global accessibility

AfriMarketRwa has the potential to transform digital commerce in Africa by:
Creating trusted digital marketplaces
Connecting multiple industries into a unified ecosystem
Empowering farmers and small businesses
Reducing fraud in online transactions
Enabling cross-border services
The platform aims to support millions of users across Africa.

Future improvements will include:
Advanced dispute resolution mechanisms

11. Conclusion
AfriMarketRwa represents a new generation of decentralized digital infrastructure designed specifically for African service ecosystems.
By combining smart contract escrow payments, modular service architecture, and decentralized governance, the platform aims to build a trusted environment for digital commerce and service delivery.
